## Why I am building it

I wanted filtration and background noise in a compact device for my dorm room. I also wanted to understand the air quality in my room, especially while 3D printing: cleaning the air and learning more about the fumes both matter to me. This design starts with particulate monitoring; determining how hazardous the fumes are remains a separate question.

## Design

The concept combines a reused 120 mm fan, a filter, a particulate-matter sensor, and a custom controller. I want the fan to respond to sensor readings and eventually indicate when the filter needs replacing. The housing will likely use laser-cut panels with 3D-printed fixtures.

The schematic is finished. I am still deciding on the PCB layout and mechanical design before building the purifier.
