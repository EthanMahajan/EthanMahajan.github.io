Microplastics Within 3D Printed Cups

AP Research study · 2025 · Independent experimental research

I investigated how filament type and print settings affect particles observed in water held in FDM-printed cups. The procedure used 300 mL of water, a five-minute contact period, and microscopic particle counting, followed by analysis of patterns across the tested conditions.

The observed relationship between infill and particle presence differed by material: the report describes a negative trend for PLA and a positive trend for TPU. Its limitations include control-sampling problems and manual microscopy; polymer identity was not confirmed with FTIR. These results should be read as exploratory observations, rather than certification that a printed cup is safe for food contact.
