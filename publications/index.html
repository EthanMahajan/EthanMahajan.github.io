Research

An exploratory microscopy study of particles observed in water held in FDM-printed cups.

Particles observed in water held in printed cups

Independent AP Research study · 2025

How do filament type and print settings relate to particles observed in water held in FDM-printed cups? I investigated this question through printed samples, manual microscopy, and comparison across the tested conditions.

Read research report (PDF, 0.7 MB)

Method

The procedure used 300 mL of water, a five-minute contact period, and microscopic particle counting. The full report documents the sample preparation and analysis.

Observations

The reported relationship between infill and particle presence differed by material: a negative trend for PLA and a positive trend for TPU. These are observations from this study, not a general material-performance rule.

Limitations

Control-sampling problems and manual microscopy limit the interpretation. Polymer identity was not confirmed with FTIR. The observations do not establish certified material identification or food-contact safety.

Full report

Ethan Mahajan. Microplastics Within 3D Printed Cups. AP Research, 2025, pp. 1–30.
